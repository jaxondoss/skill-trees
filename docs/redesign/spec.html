1. The idea

Every logged goal becomes light travelling down a tube into the statue, and that is the only light in the room.

The room is dark and still. Nothing glows unless it was earned. The statue is obsidian; effort fills its cracks with gold, the way kintsugi mends a broken bowl, so a break is repaired in the open, never hidden. That is the stoic reading, and it is also the rule that keeps the design honest: light is information.

Competitor test: Whoop, Oura and Apple Fitness show rings and charts. Only FORGE has a statue you light and repair. Remove the statue and this could be any dashboard; keep it at the centre and everything else supports it.

Rules that follow from the idea

  1. Light is earned. Glow appears only on the figure, its veins and its tubes. Nothing else glows: not cards, not text, not buttons, not the background.
  2. Gold means you. Gold marks the overall score, the tier, the veins and days at 85% or more. Never a button, an icon, a heading or decoration.
  3. Colour means a category. The five category colours appear only on data: tubes, chart marks, check fills, the bar on a schedule item. Interface chrome is navy, bone and grey.
  4. Stillness at rest. The page does not move by itself. Motion answers an action (a pulse, a pour, a crack, a tier-up) and then stops.
  5. No guilt. Misses are absence of light, never red alarms. Red appears only as Athletics' colour.

2. How the content is organized

The layout follows the dichotomy of control. It is shown by placement, never by labels.

Zone Question it answers Modules
Right rail What is in my control today? Today checklist, due soon, today's schedule
Centre What does it add up to? Score strip, the figure with five category tubes, trends
Below What is the record? Completion calendar, bottle archive, activity log

On a laptop all three zones share the first screen: the strip, the figure and the start of the Today list are visible without scrolling. The record is one scroll down.

5a. Editing and logging (crucial: it has to be effortless)

The dashboard is where you act, so nothing should send you to another page to change a goal or record a number. Every edit and every log happens in place, and every one can be undone.

Editing a goal's wording

  • Where: in the Today list. On a laptop, double-click the goal's text, or focus the row and press E. On the phone, long-press opens the goal sheet, which now starts with an Edit row.
  • What you can change: the goal's title and its Floor, Standard and Stretch wording and minutes, in one compact form inside the row (laptop) or the sheet (phone). The form is filled with the current wording, focused on the field you tapped, and saves on Enter or on blur.
  • Scope, asked once in the form: "Today only" or "From now on". Today only changes this plan's copy (plan_goals.title and text). From now on also updates the daily goal (non_negotiables), so future plans use it; today's row updates too if it has not been logged.
  • Category plates and skill names are renamed the same way from the plate's menu; the skill page keeps its full editor for everything else (days, intention, budget).

Logging progress

  • Done in one tap stays the default: tap the circle, the goal logs at its planned tier, the pulse runs.
  • Partial progress: a goal with a measurable target (minutes, grams, miles, pages) shows a small progress tube under its text. Drag it, tap a preset (Floor, Standard, Stretch), or type a number. The tier is worked out from the value against the goal's targets: 25 of 40 minutes is Floor, 40 is Standard, 60 is Stretch. Goals without a number keep the three tier buttons.
  • Numbers on the dashboard: every number card is also its input. Click the weight card, type 184.2, press Enter; the sparkline extends with the new point. Same for sleep (bed and wake taps), chess rating, store revenue, a run's miles, and a top set.
  • Quick log is a journal, saved to Mind (decided 2026-10-17). A Journal button (the centre of the phone's tab bar; the sidebar on a laptop; or press L) opens a small sheet: a rotating stoic prompt ("What is in your control right now?", "What did you do well today?"), one free-text entry, and an optional "Head right now" choice: Heavy, Strained, Steady, Clear, Locked in. Save files it under Mind, adds a row to Activity, and sends a small pulse up the Mind tube. Numbers are not typed here; they go in their cards. The Journal card on the dashboard shows the latest entries; the Mind category page keeps the full log.
  • Undo: every log and edit shows one quiet line for 6 seconds, "Logged Store work at Standard. Undo". Undo restores the previous value exactly.
  • The 30-second rule still holds: a full day of goals is one tap each, and any number is one click, the value, and Enter.

Data this needs (build phase 3)

  • non_negotiables: optional unit and numeric targets per tier (floor_target, standard_target, stretch_target); minutes already exist and become the default target when the unit is minutes.
  • plan_goals: progress_value for partial logs, and today-only title and text edits (columns exist).
  • New journal_entries table (user, day by the 4 AM rule, created_at, body up to 1,000 characters, optional head 1 to 5), owner-only RLS like every other table; shown on the dashboard and the Mind page, included in the export.

6. Visual system

6.1 Colour tokens

:root {
  color-scheme: dark;
  --ground: #070a12;        /* navy-black: 70 to 90% of every screen */
  --surface: #0e1422;       /* cards: one tone step up */
  --raised: #141b2d;        /* hover, inputs, the open sheet */
  --edge: rgba(170, 190, 255, 0.14); /* the 1px lit top edge, cards only */
  --ink: #e6e9f0;           /* cool bone; never pure white */
  --muted: #8c95a8;         /* secondary text; 6.1:1 on --surface */
  --gold: #d4af37;          /* score, tier, veins, 85% days */
  --cat-business: #c8850e;  /* tuned from #e8a33d */
  --cat-school: #4676f2;    /* tuned from #4a7bf7 */
  --cat-athletics: #e3464b; /* tuned from #e5484d */
  --cat-mind: #a966e5;      /* tuned from #a77bff, nudged toward orchid */
  --cat-nutrition: #00a75d; /* tuned from #2fbf71 */
}
  • Category colours keep their hues; lightness is moved into the dark-mode band (OKLCH L 0.48 to 0.67) and Mind is nudged toward orchid. Validated with the dataviz checker on --surface: lightness, chroma, colour-blind separation and contrast all pass for adjacent pairs.
  • Fixed chart order: Business, School, Athletics, Mind, Nutrition. School blue and Mind violet are too close to sit next to each other (they fail when compared directly), so they are never adjacent, and every category mark carries a direct label. The mockup shows original and tuned colours side by side.
  • Contrast (computed): ink on ground 16.3:1, ink on surface 15.1:1, muted on surface 6.1:1, muted on raised 5.7:1, gold on surface 8.7:1.

6.2 Type

  • Inter for everything, tabular numerals on every number (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums).
  • Five sizes: 12 (meta), 14 (interface), 16 (reading), 28 (card figures), 56 (overall score).
  • Numbers at 28 and 56 in weight 300 to 400; tracking -0.02em at 28, -0.035em at 56. Light weights read strong on dark.
  • Titles in sentence case at 14px weight 500. No uppercase tracked labels, no eyebrows.
  • Variant A: Fraunces for tier names ("Forged") and identity lines only. Variant B: Inter only. Recommendation after the mockup: see section 12.

6.3 Surfaces and shape

  • Card: --surface fill, 1px --edge on the top only, radius 14px, padding 20px. No backdrop blur, no drop shadow, no outline. Nested panels are not allowed.
  • Chips and buttons: fully round, neutral fill (--raised), ink text. The only filled light control is a checked goal, filled with its category colour.
  • The motif: the tube, a rounded stroke with a soft elbow. At small scale it is the progress bar, the schedule item's left bar, the active nav marker and the line in charts.

6.4 Focus and states

  • Focus: 2px --ink ring with 2px offset on :focus-visible, everywhere (the app has none today).
  • Hover raises the surface one step (--raised); press scales to 0.98 for 140ms.

7. The figure

7.1 Assets

Generated with Higgsfield (Nano Banana Pro, 2k, 4 credits): a tier I statue (obsidian, front view, cool blue rim light, flat navy-black background) and a tier V edit of it with gold kintsugi seams. The two renders differ in reflections by about 12% of lit pixels, so all three layers are cut from tier V alone, which makes them line up exactly:

Layer How it is made Size
statue-base.webp Tier V with the gold painted out (floating-point normalized convolution over a slightly widened gold mask); faint dark seams remain, the cracks before they are filled 38 KB
statue-veins.webp Tier V's gold pixels with alpha from the gold mask 37 KB
statue-bloom.png The gold mask blurred wide in floating point, gold, with a clean floor so it never tints the background 150 KB

The stage background is the image's own background colour (#0d1018), so the picture has no edge.

7.2 How tiers and categories light it

  • Tier: veins grow outward from the heart. An elliptical mask centred at the heart (47%, 30% of the image) opens with the tier: I 0%, II 30%, III 58%, IV 92%, V everything. Gold spreading from the centre is the repair happening.
  • Category: the vein layer is clipped into seven polygons (head; left arm; right arm; chest; core; left leg; right leg) assigned to the five regions (head: Mind; left arm: Business; right arm: School; core and left leg: Athletics; chest and right leg: Nutrition, matching categories.region). Each piece's opacity is 0.55 plus 0.45 times its category score.
  • Aura: a soft gold radial behind the statue, opacity by tier, drawn in CSS (static).
  • Tubes: from each category node to its region on the statue, a 2px bright core over an 8px soft stroke in the category colour, opacity by category score. No SVG blur filters.
  • Tier-up: the radial mask opens to the new tier over 1.6s while the veins brighten; existing sound.

7.3 Fallback

Until the images load, or if they fail, the current drawn SVG figure renders with the new palette. The three layers total about 225 KB at 805 by 1200; a 2x set can follow if they look soft on a retina laptop.

8. Charts

Rules from the dataviz skill, applied: - Score trend: area with a 2px line, gold (it is the score), recessive grid, crosshair tooltip. - Today by category: donut with 2px gaps between segments, direct labels with counts, total in the centre. - Completion, 30 days: bars in the completion ramp, 4px rounded tops, 85% reference line in gold. - Training: weekly miles as bars in Athletics; lifts as sparklines with the current top set. - Sparklines: 2px line, no axes, last point marked, value beside it in ink. - One axis per chart, never dual. Values and labels in ink or muted, never in the series colour. - Every chart has a hover tooltip and a table view for screen readers.

8.1 Completion colour (replaces spec 9.2)

Red-to-green read as guilt and passed through muddy olive. Completion is now one cool hue that brightens with effort (decided 2026-10-17), and a gold line wraps each run of streak days: the line encloses consecutive days at 85% or more, opening where the run continues into the next cell, so a streak reads as one gold-bordered shape across the week:

Completion 1 to 24% 25 to 49% 50 to 69% 70 to 84% 85% and up
Fill #385573 #44698f #5b90c7 #79b0e8 #afd5fe
Day number ink ink ground ground ground

No plan and future days stay --surface; 0% on a planned day is --raised (stone, unlit). The ramp passes the ordinal checks (single hue, even lightness steps, light end clears the surface).

9. Motion and sound

Moment Motion Sound
Goal checked: the power-up A bead of light in the category colour accelerates down its tube (520ms). On impact: gold surges through the veins from the entry point (an expanding clip over a full-brightness vein and bloom layer, 1.1s); comic speed lines burst behind the statue and fade (620ms); the halftone field flashes; the statue takes the hit, a 1.8% pump upward (520ms); a comic caption pops at the region, black on bone with a hard offset shadow in the category colour ("Strength +0.6"). Then the region settles brighter, because its score went up tink, pitch rising with each goal that day
Bottle fills Existing pour pour
Streak breaks Existing crack, then the seam fills gold over 1.2s crack
Tier up Full splash: the vein reach opens to the new tier over 1.6s, a long speed-line burst, the statue pumps 3%, a large comic caption "Tier III: Forged" three rising tinks, then the bell
Hover, press Surface step 180ms; press 0.98 for 140ms none

At rest nothing moves. The power-up is loud on purpose (Jaxon: "like something out of a comic book, this guy is getting pumped with the gold veins of truth"), and it is earned: it only happens when you act. All of it is transform, opacity and clip on pre-baked layers; there are no live blur filters. With reduced motion: no travel, no speed lines, no pump; the veins brighten once instead. Hover a goal or a plate (laptop): every other tube dims and that category's tube and veins light up, so you see what the goal feeds before you log it.

10. Copy

All rules stand: no em dashes, direct, no hype, no guilt, sentence case, Intl dates ("Oct 15", never "2026-10-15"), loading labels end with an ellipsis ("Saving…"). Module titles are plain nouns: Today, Due soon, Schedule, Score trend, Completion, Training, Numbers, Calendar, Archive, Activity.

11. Performance and accessibility budget

  • Mid-range phone: 60fps scroll. No backdrop-filter, no animated SVG filters, animate transform and opacity only. Figure images under 250KB each at phone size.
  • 44px targets everywhere; axe has no serious findings; every chart has a text alternative.
  • Desktop first paint shows the strip, figure and Today without layout shift (reserved image sizes).

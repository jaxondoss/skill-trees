The statue is three aligned layers cut from one tier V render, stacked in the hero tile in this order:

1. `statue-base.webp`: the obsidian figure with the veins inpainted out.
2. `statue-veins.webp`: the gold veins alone, clipped to the tier's reach (I none, II 30, III 58, IV 92, V 160 percent of the radius from the chest) and brightened per body region at 0.55 + 0.45 times that category's score.
3. `statue-bloom.png`: the soft glow of the veins, blended with `screen`.

All three share one size and anchor; never scale them separately. The statue is the only glowing thing on the board.
